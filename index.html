FEATURED STORY · NOVEMBER 11, 1940

Saving a Life on Lake Puckaway

When the sudden Armistice Day blizzard struck Wisconsin, temperatures dropped 50 degrees within hours. Winds whipped Lake Puckaway into freezing whitecaps. Local resident Marvin Strahota ventured onto the lake in his wooden skiff, rescuing stranded hunters from the frozen marsh reeds.

Marquette Historical Society, Inc.

Founded in February 1998 as an educational 501(c)(3) nonprofit and local history affiliate of the Wisconsin Historical Society. The society preserves, advances, and shares the heritage of the Village and Town of Marquette.
